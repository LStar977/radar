# Radar

Radar finds customers for any business. It reads public signals across the web (recommendation requests, complaints about competitors, permits, new office openings, bids), works out who needs what and where, and delivers those leads filtered to the business's geography.

## Status

Concept stage.

- `site/`: interactive marketing website starring Echo, the bat companion animated in Rive. See `site/README.md` to run, rebuild or deploy it.
- `app/`: clickable app prototype with onboarding, live lead feed, AI reply drafts, pipeline, map, insights, settings and an Ask Echo chat. See `app/README.md`.
- `docs/radar-kickoff.html`: platform blueprint, the first four brand directions and the Rive companion brief.

The brand now follows Echo's look: blackberry, orchid, petal pink and moonlight, with the tagline "Find tomorrow's customers today."
